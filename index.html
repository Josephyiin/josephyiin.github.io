    Materials and defense

    Field-deployed AI for military materials testing

    Human signalTechnicians' field workflow and scientists' ASTM rating standard

    I built MOSS, a web and iOS app that rates corrosion on metal test panels from photos. Scientists use it at the U.S. Army's Florida test site and at PPG.

    I led and delivered partner-facing AI systems for PPG, U.S. Army DEVCOM ARL, and ASM International, coordinating 100+ engineers, scientists, and PMs to produce military performance standards, open data, and production-ready applications (arl.wpi.edu). One system is under commercialization.

    Representative paperMOSS: AI Platform for Discovery of Corrosion-Resistant Materials CIKM 2023 · demo video

    Data-efficient learning from expert labels

    Human signalExperts' edge annotations, built into the loss

    We released an open corrosion image data set: 600 panels with expert ratings from 10 years of lab testing, where trained non-experts failed to match the experts. Corrosion boundaries are thin and labeled images are few. I then designed edge-guided and class-balanced losses that use experts' edge annotations, so a lightweight U-Net outperforms Transformers on this small, imbalanced dataset.

    Representative papersCorrosion Image Data Set for Automating Scientific Assessment of Materials BMVC 2021
    DeepSC-Edge: Scientific Corrosion Segmentation with Edge-Guided and Class-Balanced Losses ICMLA 2023

    Sim-to-real transfer

    Human signal1 to 3 expert-labeled field examples

    Labeled corrosion images are plentiful from indoor lab tests but scarce from outdoor field tests. We trained on indoor images and used domain adaptation to rate outdoor images without outdoor labels, improving accuracy by 27% over a no-adaptation baseline. We then ensembled independently trained adaptation models using only 1 to 3 labeled target examples, beating the prior best semi-supervised results on DomainNet (+8.8%, 1-shot) and Office-31 (+15.8%, 1-shot).

    Representative papersTransferring Indoor Corrosion Image Assessment Models to Outdoor Images via Domain Adaptation ICMLA 2022
    Ensembles of Statistically Independent Models: A Method for Semi-Supervised Domain Adaptation ICMLA 2025

    Generative design of materials

    Human signalScientists' composition and casting conditions as prompts; outputs verified by materials scientists

    AlloyGAN generates aluminum alloy microstructure images from composition and casting conditions in seconds, replacing weeks of casting trials during early exploration.

    Representative paperAlloyGAN: Domain-Promptable Generative Adversarial Network for Generating Aluminum Alloy Microstructures ICMLA 2023

    Healthcare

    Trustworthy clinical LLMs

    Human signalHuman-annotated social needs in 1,877 clinical notes

    I fine-tuned LLMs (Llama, Mistral, DeepSeek, GPT-3.5, GPT-4o) to extract social determinants of health from 1,877 clinical notes. Fine-tuned GPT-4o reached macro-F1 0.90, up from 0.75 for a RoBERTa baseline; Mistral-7B led the open-source models at 0.82.

    Representative paperExtracting Social Determinants of Health from Clinical Notes Using Large Language Models AMIA 2025 (poster)

    Multimodal agents in healthcare

    Human signalHow clinicians combine notes, images, and signals in decisions

    A scoping review I co-authored maps how multimodal AI agents are built and evaluated in healthcare, and where clinical evidence and safety checks are still missing.

    Representative paperMultimodal Artificial Intelligence Agents in Healthcare: A Scoping Review npj Digital Medicine (Nature Portfolio) 2026
