    About me

    Hello, I'm Joseph.

    Senior AI Research Scientist · UMass Chan Medical School

    I build simple yet effective AI for edge, small, and multimodal data, deployed beyond benchmarks in real-world physical systems to profoundly save human expert labor where annotations are scarce and mistakes are costly.

    Now
    Trustworthy AI for precision medicine at UMass Chan: wearable rare-event prediction, clinical notes + EHR fusion, multimodal agents with harness, fairness-aligned LLM post-training.
    PhD
    AI for Science at WPI with U.S. Army Material Research: lightweight, data-efficient, scientist-infused vision automation for millimeter-scale corrosion perception and alloy microstructure generation, deployed and used at the U.S. Army's Florida materials testing field, PPG Inc., and startups.
    Industry
    On-device personalization for Amazon Alexa Rewrite; human cognitive screening automation invented at iFlytek Co. (issued patent with product in clinical use).
    Seeking
    Research Scientist / Engineer roles in Physical AI & Robotics, Agentic AI, LLM Post-Training, Multimodal AI, and AI for Science.
